KV cache is computed as 2 · layers · kv_heads · head_dim · context · batch · bytes, so models with grouped-query attention (most here) show the real, much smaller figure rather than the naive one. Weights use packed bits per parameter plus group scales and zero-points. Overhead is a 12% allowance for activations, fragmentation and the CUDA context. Throughput and accuracy are estimates from published post-training quantization results, including my own — treat them as planning figures, not a benchmark.
